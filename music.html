01 / About

I started playing because of a song, and stayed because of the people. Over the years music has meant a band that feels like a family, a solo project where I could try anything, and a first project that taught me how much work happens before the stage.

I write and play as a guitarist and vocalist. I am not interested in being the loudest person in the room — I am interested in the arrangement that makes the room go quiet.

"My Music, My Family."

— Corduroy
