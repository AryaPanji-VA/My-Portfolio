01 / Journey

My interest in computers started the usual way — breaking things and then wanting to know why they broke. That turned into a Computer Science degree, a habit of installing Linux on everything, and a growing focus on systems and security.

I learn by building. ORACLE OS exists because I wanted an environment that was mine — configured, understood, and reproducible. The security interest is the same instinct pointed at how systems fail.
